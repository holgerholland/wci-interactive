# WCI Interactive — Content Structure & Navigation Map

This document maps the full navigational hierarchy of the interactive application.

---

## Top-Level Sections

```
├── Overview                        (home)
├── Research                        (research)
│   ├── Environmental Action & Cleanup        (cluster-env)
│   ├── Circular Economy & Cities             (cluster-ce)
│   ├── Social Innovation & Participation     (cluster-si)
│   ├── Impact Measurement & Verification     (cluster-im)
│   └── ISO 26000 Framework                   (iso-framework)
│
├── Innovation Hub                  (hub)
│   ├── Community Projects                    (community)
│   ├── Conference / Summit                   (conference)
│   ├── We Create Future                      (wcf)
│   │   ├── Kampagne One Bag                  (wcf-campaign)
│   │   ├── Panel                             (wcf-panel)
│   │   └── Podcast                          (wcf-podcast)
│   ├── Impact Entrepreneurship Lab           (iel)
│   │   ├── Startup Programme                 (startup-prog)
│   │   ├── Innovation Workshops              (innovation-ws)
│   │   ├── Guest-Lectures                    (guest-lectures)
│   │   ├── Startup Incubation                (incubation)
│   │   └── Circular Economy Labs            (ce-labs)
│   ├── Academic Partnerships                 (academic-partners)
│   ├── NGO Collaboration                     (ngo)
│   ├── City Partnerships                     (city)
│   └── World Cleanup Certified Network       (certification)
│       ├── Certification & Onboarding        (cert-process)
│       ├── Certified Community               (cert-network)
│       └── Annual Review                    (cert-review)
│
├── Learning                        (learning)
│   ├── Project Based Learning                (pbl)
│   ├── Student Impact Projects               (sip)
│   ├── ACASOL                                (acasol)
│   └── Research Assistants                  (research-assistants)
│
├── Governance                      (governance)
├── Funding                         (funding)
└── Roadmap                         (roadmap)
```
