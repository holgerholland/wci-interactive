Research Unit · Cluster 4.4 · Sub-Programme
ISO 26000 Certification Framework Development
The WCI develops an applied certification framework based on ISO 26000 — translating its seven core subjects of social responsibility into a practical, science-backed verification path for World Cleanup organisations globally.
ISO 26000Social ResponsibilityScientific FrameworkVerification

Important Note on ISO 26000

ISO 26000 is not a classical certification standard — it is a comprehensive guidance document on social responsibility. The WCI certification programme is an applied framework based on its seven core subjects. Scientific backing from the Research Unit ensures the framework meets the highest standards of rigour and independence.

Organisational Governance

Accountability, transparency and ethical behaviour in decision-making

Human Rights

Due diligence, rights-based approaches and protection of vulnerable groups

Labour Practices

Fair conditions, health & safety and social dialogue

The Environment

Prevention of pollution, resource use and climate change mitigation

Fair Operating Practices

Anti-corruption, fair competition and responsible supply chains

Consumer Issues

Fair marketing, data protection and access to essential services

Community Involvement & Development

Education, culture, employment creation and civic engagement

Certified Network · 5.6.3
Annual Certification Review
Certification is not a one-time badge — it is a commitment to continuous improvement. The annual review ensures certified organisations maintain and advance their sustainability impact year on year.
Annual ReviewContinuous ImprovementRe-Certification

Progress Report

Certified organisations submit an annual impact report documenting progress against the commitments made during certification.

WCI Evaluation

WCI Research Unit reviews progress reports and flags areas where additional support or improvement is needed.

Re-Certification Decision

Organisations meeting the annual criteria retain their certified status; others receive a structured improvement plan.

Impact Score Update

Annual review results feed into the WCI Impact Score — both for the individual organisation and the overall institute score.
